    06 / Before we begin

    Good questions.
    Straight answers.

    Who is this studio for?

    Startups, independent creators, local businesses, and growing teams that need a clear, polished web presence. Each project is reviewed for fit and scope before work begins.

    Can you build for mobile and desktop separately?

    Yes. Layouts are designed responsively for phones, tablets, and desktop screens. Where needed, the composition and navigation adapt specifically to each screen size rather than simply shrinking the desktop layout.

    Will my contact form collect enquiries?

    The form sends enquiries to the studio API and saves them in its configured database once the backend and database are deployed and healthy. Email alerts are optional and require separate SMTP setup. The form should be tested after deployment before public promotion.

    What do you need from me?

    Your business details, target audience, offer, preferred contact email, any existing logo or imagery, and timely feedback. If you are starting from scratch, we can define the essentials during the project brief.

    Can you guarantee more sales?

    No ethical developer can guarantee sales from a website alone. The goal is to make the offer clearer, improve usability, build trust, and make it easier for the right visitors to contact you.
