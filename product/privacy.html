Privacy, in plain language.

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This page explains how Zahid Mir Studio handles information submitted through the project enquiry form.

Information collected

If you submit an enquiry, the form may collect your name, email address, business or brand name, estimated budget, project type, and the message you write.

Why it is used

The information is used to review your request, reply to you, and discuss a potential website project. It is not intended for selling personal information or sending unrelated marketing.

Storage and email

After the backend is deployed and connected to its database, enquiry details are stored in that database. Email notifications are only sent if the studio configures an email provider. If the service is unavailable, the form may not submit successfully; you can contact the studio directly at zahidmirstudio@gmail.com.

Sharing and security

Information is not intentionally sold. It may be processed by hosting, database, or email providers used to operate the service. Reasonable technical safeguards are used, but no internet service can promise absolute security. Do not submit passwords, payment card details, government identifiers, or other sensitive information through the form.

Retention and requests

Enquiry records may be kept while needed to respond and manage project discussions, then deleted when no longer needed, subject to applicable legal requirements. To request access, correction, or deletion of an enquiry, email zahidmirstudio@gmail.com and include enough information to identify the enquiry.

Changes

This notice may be updated if the form, providers, or data practices change. The date at the top indicates the latest revision.

Studio contact: zahidmirstudio@gmail.com
